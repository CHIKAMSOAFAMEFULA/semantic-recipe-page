How to Prepare Nigerian Fried Rice

nigerian fried rice is a popular and delicious meal prepared with rice, vegetables,seasonings and spices its colorful nutritious, and easy to make. This recipe will guide you through the steps to create a flavorful Nigerian fried rice dish that can be enjoyed by everyone.

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups of long-grain perboiled rice
  • 1 cup of diced carrots
  • 1 cup of green peas
  • 1 cup of choped green beans
  • 1 cup of sweet corn
  • 1 medium-sized green ball pepper
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 cooking spoons of vegetable oil
  • 2 seasoning cube
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of thyme
  • salt to taste
  • 4 cups of chicken stock or water
  • cooked chicken or any meat of choice

    • Kitchen Equipment

    • cooking pot
    • frying pan
    • wooden spoon
    • kitchen knife
    • gas cylinder
    • seive
    • chopping board

    Preparation Steps:

    1. wash the rice thoroughly with clean water to remove dirt
    2. put the rice in a pot add chicken stock or water and cook until it is almost soft.
    3. wash and dice carrot, green peas, sweet corn, green beans, ball pepper and onions into small pieces.
    4. heat vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
    5. add the chopped onion and stir-fry for about 2 minutes.
    6. add the carrot, green peas, sweet corn, green beans and ball pepper. stair-fry for about 5 minutes.
    7. add curry powder, thyme, seasoning cubes, and salt to taste. stir properly to combine the ingredients.
    8. add the cooked rice to the vegetables and mix thoroughly until the rice is evenly coated with the seasoning
    9. add the cooked chicken or meat of your choice and stir gently
    10. allow the rice to cook for another 5 minutes and stirring occasionally until all the ingredient are properly mixed and heated.
    11. remove the pot from the heat and serve while hot

    Important safety Notes

    Warning: be careful while handiling hot oil and boiling water to avoid burns

    Food Safety: Ensure that chicken and liver are thorougly cooked before adding them to the fried rice

    Tip: avoid adding too much water when cooking the rice becuase the rice may become too soft and sticky

    Understanding common cooking terms

    Parboling
    pertially cooking rice in a boiling water before completing the cooking process
    Stir-Frying
    cooking food quickly over relatively high heat while continuously stirring
    Seasoning
    Adding salt, pepper, and other flavorings to food
